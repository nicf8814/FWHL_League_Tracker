# FWHFFL League Manifesto (text copy)

Source: `docs/rules/fwhffl-manifesto-2026-10-29.pdf` (created Nov 18, 2011, updated Oct 29, 2026). This is a reference transcription for Claude; the PDF is authoritative. Not shown on the site.

## Art. I Object
Lineup of NFL players whose regular-season stats, scored per Art. IX, beat every other team.

## Art. II Teams
12 teams, 1 division.

## Art. III Roster
- Max 14 players. Any move that adds a player needs a matching move that returns to 14.
- Must be able to fill an active lineup at all times.
- 1 IR spot for IR-eligible players; can't return until roster limits are met.
- Rosters lock at the start of Week 14 (amended 2026, see App. V #3).

| Position | Starters | Max |
|---|---|---|
| QB | 1 | 4 |
| RB | 2 | 5 |
| WR | 2 | 8 |
| TE/WR | 1 | – |
| Flex (RB/WR/TE) | 1 | – |
| Bench | 6 | – |
| IR | 1 | – |

Reserve roster: max 7 players including IR.

## Art. IV Auction Draft
- First weekend before/after NFL preseason starts. Attendance mandatory.
- Each team buys 13 players with a $200 budget. Need not spend it all.
- Nomination order: previous year's reverse final standings.
- Opening bid $1, increments $1.
- Can't bid more than you can afford: max bid = money left − (open spots − 1) × $1.
- Can't bid on a player whose only position is already at its max.
- Players starting the season injured or suspended may be drafted.

## Art. V Fees
$100 dues, paid by end of the draft or the roster freezes. No side bets.

## Art. VI Player salaries
- Drafted: auction price. Undrafted free agent: $1. Free agent who was drafted: his auction price. Waiver pickup: highest winning bid.
- Salary fixed until a GLC is signed or he returns to the draft pool.

## Art. VII Prize money
1st $700; 2nd–6th get $100 back.

## Art. VIII Standings
Head to head.

## Art. IX Scoring (updated Aug 2026)
- Passing: 1 pt / 20 yds (0.05/yd), TD 6, INT −2, 2-pt 2.
- Rushing: 1.5 / 10 yds (0.15/yd), TD 6, 2-pt 2.
- Receiving: 1.5 / 10 yds (0.15/yd), TD 6, 2-pt 2.
- Misc: KR TD 6, PR TD 6, fumble recovered for TD 6, fumble lost −2.
- Decimal scoring for yards.
- **Half PPR (0.5 per reception) is in effect.** Nic confirmed Nov 18, 2026; this overrides the table above, which leaves receptions out.

## Art. X Trades
- From end of the draft to the Wednesday before Thanksgiving. No limit on number or type.
- Rosters must stay legal; never more than 15 players; trades don't change salary or contract.
- 24-hour review; any team can "intercept" with a new trade involving 1 of the traded players and 1 of the parties. Interception resets the clock.

## Art. XI Active/reserve moves
Any time; takes effect at the next transaction deadline; a demoted player must be replaced by an eligible one.

## Art. XII Free agents
- FAAB: $100 per season, blind bids, processed in batches; min bid $0; ties go to the lower-ranked team.
- Open free agency on NFL game days, run by the Commissioner.

## Art. XIII Guaranteed Long-Term Contracts (keepers)
- Any drafted or free-agent player can be kept for 1 year at +$10. Base = greater of auction price and free-agent cost.
- After a completed 1-year GLC: 2-year GLC at +$15 (year 1) then +$10 (year 2).
  Example: drafted $5 → 1-yr GLC $15 → 2-yr GLC $30, then $40.
- No 2-year GLC without completing a 1-year GLC.
- Keep any player as long as you can still fill the roster in the next draft.
- Max 2 keepers per team starting 2026 (App. IV #6).
- After the 2-year GLC the player re-enters the draft.
- Contracts transfer with trades. A dropped 2-year GLC still counts against the team that signed it; a team that claims him takes on the contract.
- Chris Henry rule: contract void if the player dies.
- Travis/Aaron Rodgers rule: keepers lock 1 week before the draft; injury, suspension or arrest after the lock lets the owner void a new 1- or 2-year GLC.
- Calvin Ridley clause: a season-long suspension does not void a 2-year GLC.

## Art. XIV Playoffs
- Top 6 make it, starting Week 14. Seeds 1–2 (byes) and 3–5 by record; seed 6 = most points for among the rest.
- Consolation bracket seeded by record.
- Tiebreak: head to head only if the tied teams played the same number of games against each other, else points for. Restart after each tie is broken.

## Art. XV Governance
Commissioner for Life (Clint Helling) interprets rules; optional 3-person Executive Committee; majority vote can veto. Rule changes by majority in the off-season; in-season needs 3/4. Commissioner can set lineups, push/kill trades and make FA pickups for others, but no waiver claims except for his own team.

Founding: Clint Helling, David Morris, Ty Carlson, Ryan Cooper, Nick Heisterkamp, Travis Mattson. Expansion: Nic Ferrara, Grant Gosselin, Tim Goebel, Chris Walker, Vince Brunoforte, Brae Jones.
